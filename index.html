About the Clinic

Specialist-Led Dentistry, Close to Home

Located at Sky Avenue, Sector 34, Kamothe, Dr. Ranpise's Dental Clinic brings superspeciality dental care to Navi Mumbai. Led by Dr. Tushar P. Ranpise, BDS, MDS (Nair Hospital) — a Prosthodontist & Implantologist and former Assistant Professor at Government Dental College, Mumbai.

From a child's first check-up to full-mouth implant rehabilitation, every treatment plan is designed with precision, honesty, and your long-term health in mind.

  • ✓ Painless single-visit RCT
  • ✓ Dental implants & prosthetics
  • ✓ Digital X-ray & sterilization
  • ✓ Crowns, bridges & dentures
  • ✓ Braces & aligners
  • ✓ Teeth cleaning & whitening

Our Treatments

Complete Dental Care Under One Roof

Dental Implants

Braces & Aligners

Kids & Family Dentistry
